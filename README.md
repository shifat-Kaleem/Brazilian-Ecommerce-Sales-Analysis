# 🇧🇷 Brazilian E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end **Brazilian E-Commerce Sales Analysis** project using the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset.

The project analyzes sales performance, customer behavior, payment methods, product categories, order status, customer locations, and customer retention to identify meaningful business insights.

The analysis was performed using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**, with an interactive dashboard created to present the key findings in a clear and business-focused format.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to understand:

- How sales performance changed over time
- Which product categories generated the highest sales
- Which Brazilian states contributed the most revenue
- Which payment methods were most commonly used
- How orders were distributed across different statuses
- The proportion of one-time vs. repeat customers
- Overall customer satisfaction based on review scores
- Key trends that can support business decision-making

# 📈 Key Insights

### 💰 Sales Growth

Sales increased significantly between 2016 and 2018.

- **2016:** 49.8K
- **2017:** 6.16M
- **2018:** 7.39M

This indicates strong growth in the e-commerce business during the analyzed period.

### 🏆 Top Product Categories

The highest-selling categories included:

1. Beauty & Health
2. Watches & Gifts
3. Bed, Bath & Table
4. Sports & Leisure
5. Computers & Accessories

### 🌎 Top Customer States

São Paulo generated the highest sales contribution, followed by:

- Rio de Janeiro
- Minas Gerais
- Rio Grande do Sul
- Paraná

### 💳 Payment Methods

Credit cards were the dominant payment method, accounting for the majority of payment value.

### 👥 Customer Retention

The analysis identified approximately:

- **93K one-time customers**
- **3K repeat customers**

The repeat customer rate was approximately **3.12%**, highlighting a potential opportunity to improve customer retention and loyalty.

### ⭐ Customer Satisfaction

The average review score was approximately **4.09/5**, indicating generally positive customer feedback.
